# BUG-012 — No Order Confirmation Email Received After Checkout

**Module:** Checkout  
**Severity:** Major  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open  
**Reported By:** Shivam Raj   

## Description
After successfully placing an order, no confirmation email is received by the registered user. The order confirmation page is displayed but no email is triggered.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Login with valid credentials
2. Add any product to cart
3. Proceed to checkout
4. Fill valid billing details
5. Select payment method
6. Click Place Order
7. Check registered email inbox

## Expected Result
Order confirmation email received with order ID, items, and total amount.

## Actual Result
No email received. Order confirmation page shown on website but no email triggered.

## Impact
Users have no email record of their order. Poor post-purchase experience and trust issue.

## Environment
- Browser: Chrome 
- OS: Windows 10
- URL: https://demo.nopcommerce.com/checkout
